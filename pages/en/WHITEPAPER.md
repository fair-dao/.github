# FAIR DAO: A Decentralized Autonomous Organization for Fair Governance
## Overview
In today's global Web3 wave, Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) are gradually becoming a core force reshaping traditional governance models. As an innovative DAO focused on fair governance, FAIR DAO is committed to building a transparent, democratic, and inclusive collaborative ecosystem through blockchain technology. It is a decentralized trading platform that connects ecosystem participants and consumers, providing a platform for ecosystem participants to demonstrate integrity and value, and a channel for consumers to file complaints, punish bad ecosystem participants, and obtain compensation.
## Governance Structure
FAIR DAO participates in governance by holding FAIR tokens, which is a Meme coin fairly issued on the Tron network's SunPump platform with no presale and no team allocation. Governance adopts a dual mechanism of participation contribution and token voting, ensuring the rights of core supporters while incentivizing actual participation behavior.
## Decision Mechanism
The organization's decision-making process is divided into three phases: proposal submission, community discussion, and voting execution. Any member who holds FAIR tokens and has staked them can submit proposals. Proposals undergo a 72-hour community discussion period, during which core contributors can provide professional opinions for improvement.
## How to Participate
FAIR DAO welcomes all individuals and organizations who agree with the concept of fair governance to join, with flexible participation methods:
### Hold Governance Tokens
Purchase through Sunpump or obtain FAIR tokens through third-party transfers, and stake the tokens in the escrow contract to become a formal governance participant. Stakers can submit proposals, participate in key decision-making votes, and also participate in appeal and contribution distribution review votes to receive rewards, encouraging long-term holding and community building.
### Contribute Professional Skills
You can gain recognition by contributing skills, and when cumulative contributions reach a certain threshold, you can receive FAIR token rewards:
* Participate in technical development. We welcome submitting PR contributions or reporting issues in every project of FAIR DAO. Please refer to [Contribution Guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Contributor Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
* Participate in content creation, community operation and other fields
